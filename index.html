A Claude powered tool that turns new commits and notes into categorized, customer-facing release notes, with search and Q&A over a team's release history.

This tool is for developers, especially low level ones who need to manage and understand their codebase effectively.

Features

  • Turns new commits and notes into categorized, customer-facing release notes.
  • Provides search and Q&A over a team's release history.
  • Helps developers manage and understand their codebase effectively.
  • Integrates seamlessly with existing development workflows.
  • Enhances team collaboration by providing a centralized view of release notes.
  • Ensures that release notes are easily accessible and searchable for all team members.

About

Patchwise is a tool designed to streamline the process of managing and understanding codebases for developers. It leverages Claude's capabilities to transform raw commits and notes into structured, customer-facing release notes, making it easier for teams to collaborate and access historical release information.
